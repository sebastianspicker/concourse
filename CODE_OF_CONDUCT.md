# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this to be a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
whatever their identity, background, experience, ability, or role. Treat people
with respect, assume good intent while still talking about impact, and keep
technical feedback specific and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and precise.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Respect privacy and the public/private boundary of this campus template.
- Accept correction, repair harm, and help keep discussions useful.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and anywhere else someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants
whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately through a
[GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/sebastianspicker/concourse-campus-kit/security/advisories/new)
with "Code of Conduct" in the title. We review reports privately and as promptly
as we can. Please do not include unrelated secrets or personal data.
