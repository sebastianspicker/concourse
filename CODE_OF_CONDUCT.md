# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of identity, background, experience, ability, or role. Treat people
with respect, assume good intent while addressing impact, and keep technical
feedback specific and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and precise.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Respect privacy and the public/private boundary of this campus template.
- Accept correction, repair harm, and help keep discussions useful.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained
disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit or
remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban
participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately through a
[GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/sebastianspicker/concourse/security/advisories/new)
with “Code of Conduct” in the title. Reports will be reviewed privately and as
promptly as practical. Do not include unrelated secrets or personal data.
