Quiet Chronograph

A refined civic timepiece for students and visitors under time pressure. Keeps the Signal Board contract—clock, Now/Next, freshness, open ledgers—while raising material quiet, type discipline, and chronometric hierarchy.

Register product Primary job what · where · is it current? Signature amber Now + time spine

What changed

Now is amber (time-critical), not cobalt. Cobalt is reserved for navigation and actions. Freshness moves to a quiet live chip so status content can stay short. Schedule rows gain a time spine that marks the next item without cards.

What stayed

Institution-first identity, horizontal nav, functional clock, paired Now/Next, open ledgers, system fonts, square geometry, no glass or entrance motion. Still answers “what, where, is it current?” in one glance.

Implementation fit

Maps onto existing tokens (signal, accent, inverseSurface), design presets, and shared primitives—no new component library. High contrast and degraded states remain authoritative.
